1. York first, from turn 1. At the start it's a rebel village (about 345 men) and Scotland races you for it; in past games they held it by turn 5-20. Whoever holds it, check its garrison in your report and take it with `besiege` (add `assault: true` when you clearly outnumber it).
2. Hold Caen. It's your only foothold on the continent and it has fallen twice to stripped garrisons. Never empty London either: a tax hike on a 33-man garrison lost it once.
3. Don't open with Rennes (strength about 2500 against your 1180) or Bruges and Antwerp (500 and 460 men, much stronger than your stacks). Portugal and others take them by turn 20-50. Check the report before any target.
4. Paris has been weak: 20-31 men on turns 5-30 in past games, 3-4 tiles from Caen. Check the report; strike only if you clearly outnumber it, once Caen is stable.
5. Scotland is the neighbour that hurts you: it besieged London at turn 36 and Nottingham at turn 187 in past games, and broke an alliance. Watch Scottish stacks near York and London every turn. An alliance is a timer, not safety.
6. Longbowmen come only from a castle: build `bowyer` (1200), then `practice_range` (2400), at Nottingham or Caen. Billmen, Armoured Swordsmen, Heavy Billmen and Dismounted English Knights are castle-only too.
7. England can't recruit Armoured Sergeants. Use Armoured Swordsmen or Dismounted Feudal Knights as the line. Cheap and useful: Billmen 230, Dismounted English Knights 530, Hobilars 280. English Knights cost 860 and 250 upkeep a turn: few.
8. Crusade: a general plus 7 other units outside a settlement. Crusaders move twice as fast.
9. Jerusalem is a rebel city across the Mediterranean. Garrison your core before turn 64 (Mongol warning).
10. The long victory needs 45 regions including Jerusalem. Keep expanding from a safe core, not a thin one.
11. Caen and the continent are across water. Armies cross only by ship: `board`, sail, `land`. Never disband the fleet you need to reach Caen. One run lost 90 turns trying to walk there.
12. A castle converts to a large town for 1600 (2 turns), a large town to a castle for 3200. Do one at a time, never with an enemy stack near. Keep Nottingham a castle for troops; make conquests cities for income.
13. Merchants stand on a resource tile (dyes, wool near London); your report's AGENT LIMITS say how many you may have.
