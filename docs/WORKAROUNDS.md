Every live workaround in Parley, and the fix at its cause. The author, 2026-11-15: "we need them fixed, not worked
around" (STANDARDS `fix-what-we-can`). A row closes only when the workaround code is deleted and the cause's
fix is proven (test, and live where it touched the game). New workarounds get a row here in the same PR.

Inventory: Gemini (agy) sweep of the bridge, `parley/`, `scripts/`, HANDOFF, ROADMAP and docs, 2026-11-15;
citations spot-checked. Line numbers are as of `205dc85`.

Groups, in working order:

- **A**: the cause is already fixed in our EOP fork (build a3e678b9): delete the workaround.

- **C**: needs a C++ fix in our EOP fork (author/M2TWEOP-library, base `gitea-ci`), then the Lua side goes.
- **D**: Parley's own seats and loop.
